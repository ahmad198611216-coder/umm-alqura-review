Grammar 1

The Passive Voice 🏭

subject+be+past participle(+ by …)
Active: A Saudi company makes this rose oil.
Passive: 🌹 This rose oil is made in Saudi Arabia.
Simple past
The Qur’an Complex was opened in 1984. 📖
Present perfect
The Two Holy Mosques have been expanded. 🕋
Future
The charity boxes will be delivered. 📦
We use the passive to focus on the product – not on who made it.
Change the verb “be” to change the tense.

Grammar 2

Comparatives & Superlatives 📊

🍯
Sukkari dates
60 SAR · 500 g
🌴
Ajwa dates
120 SAR · 1 kg · ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
🧺
Khalas dates
40 SAR · 500 g
Khalas dates are cheaper than Sukkari dates.
Ajwa dates are more expensive than Sukkari.
Ajwa dates are the most expensive of the three.
It has the best rating. (good → better → the best)
Two things → comparative: cheaper than · more expensive than
Three or more → superlative: the cheapest · the most expensive

💍 This ring is as shiny as a diamond.
👛 My bag is not as big as my sister's bag.
as + adjective + as → the two things are the SAME.
not as + adjective + as → the two things are DIFFERENT.

Grammar 4

look · smell · sound · taste + like 👃

👀

look like

That bag looks like a designer bag.
👃

smell like

Saudi coffee smells like cardamom.
👂

sound like

Your plan sounds like fun!
👅

taste like

Sukkari dates taste like caramel.
Sense verb + like + noun → one thing is similar to another.
